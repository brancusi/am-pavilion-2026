Km 0.0 · Piazzale Roma

Where the road ends.

The road from the mainland, Ponte della Libertà (1933), stops here at the city’s only vehicle terminus. Past this point everything moves by boat, by hand truck or on a back.

Km 0.3 · The load

One unit.

2.6kg

8 × 8 × 24 in, the Armenian Flag Stack’s unit: an EPS core under a plaster skim. About 80 % of its weight is the skin; see The Weight.

Km 0.6 · The pack

Four on a frame.

12.8kg

Four units on an aluminium frame; the frame itself weighs about 2.5 kg.

Km 1.23 · Bridge 7

Ponte dei Meloni is tagged as a level pedestrian way, nearly at the height of the calli (an OSM user note).

Km 1.45 · Calle Dogana da Terra

The porters’ ground.

Route A passes 10 m from the Calle Dogana da Terra, the land customs, and runs past the Salizada del Fontego dei Tedeschi on the far side of Rialto. The Fontego’s bastazi (porters) were founded in 1413 and kept their altar at San Bortolomio.

Km 1.64 · Bridge 8 · Rialto

114steps

Four flights: 18 and 42 up, 42 and 12 down. Sixty steps up, the biggest climb of the walk; after it, ↑ 123 of 182.

Km 2.0 · The climb so far

136of 182 up

Steps climbed after bridge 9. At 13–16 cm a step, about 18–22 m. The load is light on purpose. What the walk carries is the distance, the steps and the repetition.

Leve fit, quod bene fertur, onus.

A load borne well becomes light.

Ovid, Amores I.2.10 (tr. here).

How the route was measured

Route A: shortest path on OSM footways, pedestrian ways and steps (data 2026-06-01), computed 2026-10-15. Steps: OSM step_count, 37 flights, direction from incline. Distances along the route: each bridge projected onto the line. Rise per step: 13–16 cm.
