## What's in them

- **The Italian Flag:** painting green, spreading spackle; green, white and red panels standing on their
  own feet in Tesa 41; a conversation about play ("Through play is the only way to create"), roots, a
  flag "loaded with message", and carrying it to San Marco for a day. Captions burned in.
- **Carrying:** a red unit on a back frame, carried through the Arsenale and Castello at golden hour.
  Voice-over on carrying weight, representing Armenia, and Venice where everything moves by hand or boat.
- **Murano:** furnaces and glassblowers, a gallery of glass hands, and a conversation at a café table
  about Peggy Guggenheim, Adriano Berengo and a "living active studio". Captions burned in. The
  automatic transcript mishears "Adriano Berengo" and "Murano".
- **Before the Flag:** a white crenellated foam form in the hall; vertigo, scale, the roof beams, and the
  simple unit becoming an assembly. Ends on the lagoon, screen-printing, the flag panels and a red pour.
  Captions burned in.
- **In Tesa 41:** the Italian flag in its foam state, interlocking parts, pigments and brushes, Zadikian at
  the wall. One phrase may be misheard: "the air is in a way dictating us".
- **The Maquette:** Los Angeles. A 3D model on screen, then full-scale foam units; squares, rectangles and
  proportion, judged by standing next to the form.
- **Leaning Red:** Los Angeles garden studio, building a tall form from cardboard and tape, then a panel in
  a white room. Music only, no speech.
